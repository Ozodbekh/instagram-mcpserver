Privacy Policy — Sovchi.app Instagram Integration

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Sovchi.app Instagram integration receives, what we do with it, and how you can have it removed. It covers only this integration — messages and comments sent to the Sovchi.app Instagram account. It does not cover the Sovchi.app mobile application, which has its own policy.

Who we are

Sovchi.app operates a matchmaking service in Uzbekistan, distributed as a mobile application and as the Telegram bot @sovchiapp_bot. For anything in this policy, write to sovchiap@gmail.com.

What we receive

When you send a direct message to the Sovchi.app Instagram account, or comment on one of its posts, Meta notifies our server. That notification contains:

  • your Instagram account identifier and username;
  • the text of your message or comment;
  • the time it was sent, and the identifier of the post it relates to.

We receive nothing else. We do not read your other messages, your followers, your private posts, or anything on your account that you have not sent to us.

What we do with it

We use it for one purpose: to read and answer what you sent us. A notification is delivered to the Sovchi.app team so that a person can respond.

Third parties. Two are involved. Meta Platforms delivers the notification to us. Telegram carries it to the team member who answers you. Both process it under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to build a profile of you.

How long we keep it

Our servers do not store these messages. The notification is passed to the team and nothing is written to our database. A copy remains in the team's Telegram conversation and in your own Instagram inbox, and is deleted on request.

Deleting your data

Write to sovchiap@gmail.com from any address, or message @sovchiapp_bot, and say which Instagram username the request is for. We will delete every copy we hold within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

You can also revoke our access at any time from Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. Doing so stops any further notification reaching us.

Security

Notifications reach us only over HTTPS, and every one is cryptographically signed by Meta and verified by our server before it is read. An unsigned or altered request is rejected.

Children

Sovchi.app is intended for adults. We do not knowingly process information belonging to anyone under 18. If you believe we have, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will be announced through @sovchiapp_bot.
